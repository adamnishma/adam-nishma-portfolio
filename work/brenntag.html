03 / Internal marketplace

Brenntag Source

A global internal marketplace that made slow-moving stock visible across the supply chain.

The platform contributed to a €400M reduction in working capital after full rollout and connected data across 72 countries.

Role
UX/UI Designer, concept to launch
Scope
Internal marketplace, supply chain
Timeline
~4 months
Reach
72 countries connected

From requirements
to experience

Brenntag’s branches operated in isolation, buying externally while sister branches held slow-moving stock. I translated the business vision into an experience that felt as simple as B2C commerce and made the “TicketSwap for chemical stock” concept tangible enough to secure full-time funding.

  • Shaped the proactive marketplace strategy.
  • Designed Smart Match flows connecting product managers across regions.
  • Created an interface that reduced the learning curve for procurement teams.

A transparent
global marketplace

The platform sat on top of legacy systems. It standardized trust with transparent fixed-margin pricing, made global stock visible across 72 countries, and gave local branches a way to list slow-moving stock instead of letting it expire.

From wireframe
to working model

This sequence shows how the product moved from early flows and data relationships to an operational marketplace experience.

Results &
impact

The platform became a cornerstone of Brenntag’s digital transformation: unifying supply data, surfacing alternatives during the 2021 supply-chain crisis, and helping the business move existing stock instead of buying new.

  • Connected supply chain data across 72 countries.
  • Contributed to a €400M reduction in working capital after full rollout.
  • Helped procurement teams locate alternative stock sources within the group.
