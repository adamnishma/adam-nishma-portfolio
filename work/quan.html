01 / Wellness platform

Quan & TrueTribe

A wellness platform that translated complex psychological data into useful action for teams.

70% survey completion, more than twice the industry average, followed by a successful acquisition by Colbe.

Role
Principal Product Designer
Scope
Consumer + admin product, system architecture
Timeline
~4 years, through acquisition
Result
70% survey completion rate

Research &
discovery

The challenge was translating deep psychological drivers into actionable, personalized recommendations. I worked closely with domain experts and users throughout the product lifecycle, turning research into a clearer information architecture and a more useful reporting model.

  • Collaborated with an in-house psychology team to translate complex drivers into design requirements.
  • Tested features before release and used behavioral data to iterate.
  • Found that reporting was a primary retention driver through direct buyer feedback.

One platform for
wellness at work

I replaced standard forms with progressive disclosure to reduce cognitive load, separated a linear front end from complex branching logic, and structured raw data into dashboards decision-makers could act on.

The co-creation
wizard

Teams rarely commit to external advice. I shifted from a prescriptive model to a co-creative one: a three-step ritual that let teams diagnose a challenge, define an action, and build a natural-language pledge together.

Can’t play the preview? Download the co-creation example.

Making trade-offs
visible

Comparative prototyping moved debates about speed and scope from subjective negotiation to objective comparison. I designed the full North Star, the minimal MVP, and a bridge plan back toward the larger vision.

Results &
impact

  • 60% increase in user engagement with the platform.
  • 70% survey completion rate, more than twice the industry standard.
  • The matching engine and accumulated knowledge became core value drivers in the company’s acquisition.
  • A scalable design system supported years of product growth.
